Appliance Energy Consumption

Does the screen technology of a TV (LCD, LCD (LED) or OLED) change how much electricity it uses? This site answers that question with energy rating data for televisions sold in Australia.

Why energy use matters

Household appliances are a large part of a home's electricity bill. Energy rating labels help shoppers compare products, but the label alone does not show how much of the difference comes from screen size and how much from screen technology.

This site explores government energy rating data for the 4,839 televisions currently available on the Australian market. It focuses on one factor: screen technology.
