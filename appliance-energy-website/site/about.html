About Us

Who made this site, what it asks, and where the data comes from.

The project

This website was built for COS30045 Data Visualisation (Demonstration 1) at Swinburne Vietnam. It asks one question: does the screen technology of a television (LCD, LCD (LED) or OLED) change its energy consumption? It uses only televisions currently available on the Australian market.

The author

Pham Anh - 105977992 – CS student, Swinburne Vietnam.

The data

ItemDetail
DatasetEnergy rating data for labelled televisions (file: tv_2026_10_02.csv)
SourceAustralian Government energy rating product registration database (GEMS). https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/energy-rating-for-household-appliances
Size5,030 registered models and 32 columns; 4,839 models kept after filtering
FiltersAvailability Status = Available, and sold in Australia (SoldIn contains “Australia”)
Main variablesScreen_Tech, labelled energy consumption (kWh/year), screen size and area, in-use power, star rating
Data dictionarytelevisionsmay2015.docx
ToolsKNIME for filtering and cleaning

Limitations

  • Energy use is the labelled kWh per year, not measured use in a real home.
  • 228 rows share a brand and model number with another row. They are kept as separate registrations.
  • There are few LCD models at very large sizes, so comparisons for 76 inches and above are less reliable.
